AI Lab 03 · Synthetic Research and CX Discovery · Completed November 13, 2026

Stop selling empty tools.
Diagnose the revenue leak first.

As we rebrand Mercury Call Desk into Mercury CRM, I used synthetic user research to find our highest-leverage B2B personas and pressure-test our onboarding flow. Walking 40 synthetic buyers through a standard 14-day DIY trial exposed a 65% drop-off at technical setup, leading us to replace the blank trial with a 60-second live AI phone test and a 5-day Revenue Diagnostic.

How I approached it

The decisions that shaped the work

  • Ground personas in evidence, not guesses.I configured ux-persona-builder with an evidence linter so every persona claim maps to MIT/RAND studies, Reddit threads, or G2 reviews—and flagged freebie-hunting blog traffic to exclude from ad spend.
  • Converge on volume, diverge for leverage.Convergence identified busy inbound sales managers (Mortgage/Lending) and home service crews, while divergence surfaced B2B systems consultants who bring 5 to 20 client sub-accounts at once.
  • Expose onboarding friction at scale.Walking 40 synthetic buyers through a standard 14-day DIY trial revealed a 65% drop-off when asking busy owners for phone carrier API keys and a blank AI prompt box.
  • Lead with a diagnostic and live proof.In split testing, a 60-second live AI phone call paired with a 5-day Revenue Diagnostic beat the empty DIY trial across our core revenue personas.
