From the lab to the proof

Every lab follows the same structure, so you can go as deep as you want.

  1. The lab

    The problem, the case and what I decided, on one page.

  2. The recording

    My presentation of the work, in my own voice, as submitted.

  3. The deliverable

    The decks, dashboard or app itself, live or downloadable.

  4. The proof

    Scripts, prompts, briefs, claim registers and verification notes as Markdown files.
