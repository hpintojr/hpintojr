# Leadership & GTM Brief · Mercury CRM CX Discovery

**Author:** Hamilton Pinto Jr.  
**Subject:** B2B Persona Discovery, Onboarding Usability Audit & Diagnosis-First Offer Pivot  
**Date:** November 13, 2026  

## 1. Executive Summary
As Mercury Call Desk rebrands into **Mercury CRM**, our primary commercial question is how to allocate marketing capital and design our onboarding funnel so B2B prospects convert into long-term recurring accounts. Using the reusable `ux-persona-builder` skill and synthetic user testing across 40 B2B buyer profiles, we uncovered a **65% drop-off** in traditional self-serve "14-Day Free Trial" onboarding caused by carrier API configuration and blank-canvas prompt fatigue.

**Recommendation:** Replace the blank DIY trial with a **60-Second Live AI Phone Sandbox + Paid 5-Day Revenue Diagnostic**, and concentrate acquisition budget on two high-leverage segments:
1. **High-Volume Inbound Sales Floors** (*Speed-to-Lead Sam* — Mortgage, Lending, Debt, Legal).
2. **B2B Systems Consultants & Niche Micro-Agencies** (*Agency Architect Alex* — deploying 5–20 client sub-accounts via 1-click industry snapshots).

## 2. Key Findings from Convergent & Divergent Persona Discovery
- **Obvious Volume ICP (Converge):** Inbound Sales Floor Directors (*Speed-to-Lead Sam*) and Local Multi-Crew Operators (*Field-Crew Carlos*) experience acute revenue loss whenever after-hours calls hit voicemail. However, they abandon self-serve software that requires telecom engineering or prompt writing on Day 1.
- **Non-Obvious Multiplier ICP (Diverge):** B2B Systems Consultants (*Agency Architect Alex*) represent a smaller share of raw top-of-funnel traffic but deliver 5x–20x the LTV by cloning vertical workflows across their entire client roster.
- **Traffic Trap Excluded:** Solo hobbyists searching for "free AI voice tools" (*Freebie-Hunter Sid*) consume content but do not convert; excluding them from paid targeting preserves CAC efficiency.
