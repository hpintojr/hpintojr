### Speaker notes

The owner selected six hours as the marketing default. The website and marketing playbooks state six hours, while FAQ p. 7 states up to four hours and references UL designs. We have not independently reviewed those listings or a six-hour test report. Keep the warning visible. Never represent a referenced standard as product certification. Sources: https://www.xbeton.com/; XBeton-FAQ-Guide.pdf (2025 edition) p. 7

## Card 6

Thermal and acoustic performance need assembly context
Confirm declared thermal conductivity and thickness. Keep steady-state thermal resistance separate from thermal-mass effects. Review the tested acoustic assembly, finishes and junctions.

### Speaker notes

The FAQ mixes nominal and effective thermal values, so this briefing does not turn those into a design table. Request documented properties and an appropriate assembly calculation. For sound, obtain the relevant test report and consider flanking paths. Sources: XBeton-FAQ-Guide.pdf (2025 edition) pp. 8-10, 18; https://build.xbeton.com/specifiers
