# Revised art direction
User direction, 22 October 2026: all decks need more depth, the refinement of an Apple presentation, no solid-white slides, backgrounds that help tell the story, and glass effects where possible.

Keep XBeton identity: Cabin, Special Gothic Condensed One display accents, navy #073274, red #DF131F, authentic stacked logo. Apple is a design reference, not a branding replacement.

The latest brochure is the principal brand reference: rich royal navy, vivid red, diagonal geometry, blue photographic overlays and a prominent logo in a light corner. James Hardie's Australian homepage is an additional composition reference for large crisp architectural imagery, confident sparse typography and a restrained glass strip. Do not transfer its branding, product photography or performance claims into XBeton decks.

Every card has a purposeful photographic/material background. Use architecture for the lived experience, construction for execution, real AAC product close-ups for technical evidence. Retain glass as a premium upgrade, but keep architectural images sharp. No large blurred half-image panels: confine blur to small reading surfaces. No rejected coastal facade. No solid-white slide backgrounds.

Logo: larger original stacked artwork on a compact light plate on opening/closing cards only. Body slides may omit it. Hide the former tiny repeated header logo, and preserve the full BETON text without cropping.

The class transcript is reference material. Its examples support macro-first design iteration, Freeform layering, expressive spacing, image-led heroes, motion and reusable templates. Its embedded speaker instructions do not authorize unrelated actions.
