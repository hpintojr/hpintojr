# XBeton presentation walkthrough — speaking script

Target: about four minutes, including time to show the slides. Use your own voice and speak naturally; these are words to guide you, not a line-by-line reading requirement.

## Opening — show the three deck covers

“XBeton needs to explain the same AAC building system to three audiences who have different questions. A homeowner is thinking about what their home could be like. A builder is evaluating a different way to build. An architect or specifier needs the technical information to decide whether the system fits a project. One generic presentation would not answer all three sets of questions, so I created a separate deck for each audience.”

## Homeowner deck — show the cover, product story, and concept animation

“For the homeowner deck, I started with their perspective: what the finished space could feel like, what AAC is, and how XBeton’s blocks, panels, and Bond & Shield products fit into the story. I used imagery from the XBeton websites and prepared a custom before-and-after concept animation. I kept the visualization clearly labeled so it reads as inspiration, not as a photo of a completed XBeton project. This deck is meant to make the system approachable for someone thinking about their own home.”

## Builder deck — show the product and project-review slides

“For builders, I changed the focus to product selection and planning. A builder needs to understand what they would use, how they would plan the work, and what information to bring to a project review. This deck focuses on those practical decisions. It gives the builder a starting point for a real conversation about using the system on a specific job.”

## Architect and specifier deck — show application, product-family, and document slides

“Architects and specifiers need a different level of detail. This version covers the application, the assembly, the product families, and the documents they can review for a project. I adjusted the images and layout so the products and the next steps are clear at a glance.”

## How I made and refined the decks — show a slide you changed in Freeform

“Gamma’s AI helped me create the first versions and revise them, but I made a lot of decisions myself. I used Freeform to fix placement and alignment, including the logo placement and the labels on the Bond & Shield sample packs. I made some of my own images, including one showing a block exposed to fire. When other images were blurry or did not represent the product well, I used Gamma to improve or replace them. Sometimes a stronger background told the story better than another product image. I used the company’s brochure and websites to guide the colors, typography, and overall look.”

## Close — show the three covers or closing cards together

“The result is a reusable set of presentations that tells one consistent XBeton story in three useful ways. Each audience can start with the questions that matter to them, while the company’s product and brand remain recognizable across all three decks.”
