- `https://www.xbeton.com/web/image/2158-066d7179/Industrial%20Facility%203.png`
- `https://www.xbeton.com/web/image/2223-26279c54/Industrial%20Facility%203.webp`

The only logo to display as separate brand artwork is the approved stacked red/navy XBeton SVG (`brand-assets/logos/X-Beton Logo.svg`), presented at readable size in a rounded translucent glass plaque on opening and closing cards. The owner subsequently requested the earlier authentic XBeton product photos back on specifier card 3; those source photos may include marks printed on product packaging. This is a specific exception for that card, not approval of the rejected factory image.

Approved high-resolution alternatives available for architectural context:

- XBeton Build hero house: `https://build.xbeton.com/media/hero-house.jpg` (1920 × 1076; concept architecture)
- Las Vegas Metro PD architecture reference: `https://www.xbeton.com/web/image/1853-1319a560/LAS%20VEGAS%20METRO%20PD%20%282%29.jpg` (1920 × 1180)

Captions and notes must identify these as architectural inspiration or AAC architecture references. The deck must not imply XBeton built a third-party building. Image quality is checked at presentation size in final PNG exports; the original product renders are used at their 1536-pixel source resolution.
