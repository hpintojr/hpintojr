AI Lab 01 · Storytelling & Slide Decks with AI · Completed October 2026

One company, three audiences.
Three decks that each answer a different question.

XBeton sells an AAC (autoclaved aerated concrete) building system. Homeowners, builders and architects all ask different questions about it, so one generic deck would not work for any of them. I built a separate ten-card deck for each audience in Gamma, kept one brand and one evidence register across all three, and recorded a walkthrough explaining the choices.

How I approached it

The decisions that shaped the work

  • Split the audience first.Homeowners care about comfort and design, builders about scope and crew planning, specifiers about evidence and assembly detail. Each deck opens with that audience's question and closes on its own next step.
  • One evidence register for all three decks.Every quantified claim was checked against the source documents. The six-hour fire rating conflicts with the 2025 FAQ (four hours), so it stays visibly marked [NEEDS VERIFY] on every deck that mentions it.
  • AI drafted, I directed.Gamma generated the first versions from detailed briefs. Sixteen rounds of saved prompts then fixed the theme, logo, image sharpness and labels, and I finished placement in Freeform.
  • Built to reuse.The brand system, prompts and module plan are portable. Swap the brand variables, evidence and calls to action to build a new client's decks.
