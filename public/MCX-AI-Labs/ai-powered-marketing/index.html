How I approached it

The decisions that shaped the work

  • Give readers an audio option.Use a conversational host and guest to unpack the inventory figure and the thesis in everyday terms.
  • Use a familiar story to explain the product.Move from mid-century newspaper visuals and a 2002 iMac to a navy Mercury campaign about calls, bookings, and follow-up. Place animated caption phrases on the narration beats.
  • Give each voice a role.Use ElevenLabs MCP for the host and guest, with separate voice direction for the campaign.
  • Show how the work was made.Gemini 4.0 Neon informed the specification, Muse helped revise the commercial brief, and Codex used ChatGPT 6.2 Sol at high reasoning. The usage ledger records the measured Codex data and the Claude Opus 5.5 and Ollama DeepSeek editing credits.
